# Privacy Rules (DRAFT)

Ye hamare privacy ke waade hain. Koi feature inke khilaf nahi ja sakta.

## Hum KABHI nahi karte
1. **Koi cookie nahi**
2. **Koi IP store nahi**
3. **Koi search history nahi**
4. **Koi tracker nahi** (no analytics, no Google Fonts)

## Hum kya karte hain
- Request process karke data discard.
- AI answers mein user ki pehchaan nahi bhejte.
- Sirf title + snippet + link dikhate hain.

## Legal
- India: DPDP Act 2023
- Europe: GDPR
